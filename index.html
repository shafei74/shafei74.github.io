01 About

I’m a pharmacist who turns dermatological science into clear, engaging stories — in Egyptian Arabic — so evidence-based skincare and haircare make sense to everyone.

As a pharmacist, I work from the evidence — ingredient studies, mechanisms of action and dermatological guidance. As a creator, I build everything that makes that evidence watchable: the hook, the script, the visuals and the edit.

The result is short-form content that explains what an ingredient actually does, who it’s for and how to use it safely — without hype, and without losing the science.

05 Creative Process

From research to release.

01 / 07
  1. 01

    Scientific Research

    Every topic starts with the literature — ingredient studies, mechanisms of action and current dermatological guidance.

  2. 02

    Medical Fact-Checking

    Claims, concentrations and safety notes are checked against pharmaceutical references before a line is written.

  3. 03

    Creative Concept Development

    Finding the angle: the question people actually ask, and the visual idea that answers it.

  4. 04

    Scriptwriting & Storytelling

    Turning the science into a tight, conversational Egyptian Arabic script with a clear hook and takeaway.

  5. 05

    Filming & Production

    Shot with intention — lighting, framing, product shots and clean sound.

  6. 06

    Professional Editing

    Pacing, on-screen text, motion graphics and scientific animation that make complex ideas easy to follow.

  7. 07

    Content Publishing & Analysis

    Publishing, then reading the data — views, shares, follows — to shape the next piece.
